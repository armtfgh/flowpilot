## (2) Why These Conditions Were Chosen

### Residence time and flow rate
The 34.5 min residence time and 0.0553 mL/min flow rate are derived from the KRICT experimental anchor (10–12% yield at 34–38 min) and the intensification mandate. The batch protocol requires 15 h at 40 °C; the target is a 20× residence time reduction. Class-table kinetics for photoredox (DOI: **10.1039/c9re00285e**; Knowles group, continuous-flow photocatalysis) suggest τ ~30–45 min is achievable with sufficient mass transfer enhancement. The 34.5 min point represents the upper feasible limit of the selected 0.75 mm ID coil at the minimum practical flow rate, balancing residence time with hardware constraints.

### Wavelength and light source
448 nm matches the batch protocol (450 nm blue LED). The manual strip-LED photoreactor was selected over the higher-irradiance Vapourtec system (355 mW/cm² at 450 nm, 10 mL) because the 12.26 mL reactor volume provides ~2× longer residence time — critical for this photon-dose-limited, catalyst-free system where total photon exposure (irradiance × time) is the governing variable.

### Tubing material and ID
FEP is mandatory for photochemical reactions due to its transparency across the visible spectrum. The 0.75 mm ID was selected (over 1.0 mm) to minimize the inner filter effect: at 0.5 M and Beer–Lambert path length 0.75 mm, absorbance A = 0.75 (82% photon absorption near the wall, moderate inner filter). At 1.0 mm ID, A = 1.0 (90% absorption), increasing photon loss to the walls relative to the tube core. The 0.75 mm ID also gives a mixing ratio r_mix = 0.068 (τ_mixing/τ_residence), confirming mixing is not rate-limiting.

### Gas–liquid system and BPR
Taylor slug flow in FEP tubing is the standard approach for aerobic photooxidations in flow. The O₂/liquid segmented regime maximizes gas–liquid interfacial area and kLa. The BPR at 5.0 bar maintains reactor pressure at ~6 bar absolute, giving dissolved O₂ ~37 mM in DMSO (Henry's law) — well above the stoichiometric demand. This approach is directly analogous to: **10.1039/d2re00198e** (aerobic gas–liquid photooxidation with BPR, FEP coil, Taylor slug flow) and **10.1039/d1re00496d** (continuous-flow photocatalytic aerobic oxidation with MFC O₂ feed and BPR pressure control).

### Temperature
50 °C is the maximum available for the manual strip-LED setup. Elevated temperature accelerates the thermal component of the aromatization and increases O₂ diffusivity in DMSO, improving mass transfer. The batch protocol runs at 40 °C; 50 °C provides a modest thermal boost.
