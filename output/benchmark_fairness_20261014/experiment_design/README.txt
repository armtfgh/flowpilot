Historical-method trap
The original deterministic worksheet checks Q_liquid multiplied by the declared time against reactor volume without selecting the gas-time basis. This is invalid when that declared time is an inlet/STP gas–liquid index. Its historical judge instruction treats worksheet calculations as authoritative unless the exact input/formula is challenged. The original packet builder also had fewer delivery-state fields than the current builder. Therefore, rerunning the entire old packet builder on current practical prose/structured outputs would introduce additional confounds. In particular, structured fields can be empty even when the prose states the relevant number. Reproducing that legacy worksheet is a diagnostic of the old method, not grounds to prefer the resulting score. Preserve it in byte-identical historical packets, disclose the defect, and retain raw criterion evidence; do not claim that reproducing a favorable historical number validates the method.
