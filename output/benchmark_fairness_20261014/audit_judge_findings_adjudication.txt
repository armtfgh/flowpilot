2. UO-07 OpenAI finding (unchanged recorded integer 1; critical_error=True)

Exact rationale: The delivery conflates at least three different time bases. The deterministic sheet confirms 1.569036 min only for the declared inlet/STP index, while the same outcome also claims a primary residence time of 1.569036 min despite V/Q_liquid = 6.0 min and provides conflicting in-channel values. This is a criterion-specific critical condition because reactor volume and residence-time claims contradict the executable design.

Exact requested correction: Report separate, internally consistent values for nominal liquid space time (6.0 min), inlet/STP index (1.569 min if kept), and any in-channel diagnostic basis, with one clearly designated primary basis.

Adjudication: The implication that the explicitly declared 1.569036 min inlet/STP index contradicts 3/0.5=6 min is mathematically incorrect. Both are true for their respective denominators. The canonical summary explicitly labels inlet/STP apparent residence time, and the delivered procedure supplies timing_reference_min=6.0 and calls the commissioning period three nominal liquid-volume turnovers. The worksheet also supplies nominal_volume_over_liquid_flow_min=6.0. Merely providing both indices does not demonstrate impossible reactor geometry or physically impossible residence time.

However, the same delivered packet retains an earlier model explanation calling 6 min the primary time and containing false calculations. That is a genuine documentation/rationale problem, assessed below. Calling the entire judgment a pure false positive would therefore conceal a real presentation weakness. Its canonical geometry-contradiction argument is unsupported; its concern about contradictory explanatory prose has evidence.

6. Interpretation of the near-tied aggregate

The benchmark combines independently assigned criterion integers. The OpenAI H2 evaluation both recognizes the final liquid/gas numerical closure and penalizes retained contradictory gas/time prose across several criteria. Some penalties concern a real rationale problem; the UO-07 geometry argument confuses declared time bases. Meanwhile a concrete one-shot pressure-test contradiction is missed by all three judges. Thus a small mean difference cannot be interpreted as an exact physical-quality ranking. These observations identify limitations in automated evaluation and delivered explanation quality, not grounds to replace selected grades or declare a preferred winner.

No alternative mean or hand-corrected score is calculated here. The fixed benchmark outputs and judgments should remain reportable exactly as archived, accompanied by this separate factual audit.
