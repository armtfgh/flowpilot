# Frozen Campaign

This campaign uses three cases selected before generation from the accepted five-case pilot ranking. It runs GPT-5.4 as both one-shot and FlowPilot generators, with three fresh calls per cell. Qwen, Openai, Claude independently judge every blinded outcome using the unchanged 14-criterion NewGen 2.0 rubric. Exactly 18 outcomes and 54 judgments are planned.
