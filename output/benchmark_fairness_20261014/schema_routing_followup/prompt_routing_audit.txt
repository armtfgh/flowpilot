Schema-routing audit — 2026-10-14

The fixed primary Stage B harness introduced a final-response envelope into the FlowPilot scientific objective. The observed parser data loss is deterministic; the hypothesis that this new instruction induced the model's envelope is strongly supported by all three fresh responses and the absence of that envelope from all six compared earlier responses, but cannot be separated from generation variation without the declared control.

Historical contrast
The frozen original runner's _general_one_shot independently specifies its final envelope in the one-shot call. Its _production_full uses case.objective as the design priority and does not append the one-shot envelope. The older practical campaign also supplied the plain practical objective. All three historical and all three previous practical translator replies were flat and retained their physical fields through the same current normalizer. Exact event paths and raw-response hashes are retained in ../wrapper_loss_comparison.json. This is a harness-format routing defect combined with a production parser robustness defect; it is not evidence that an extra scientific requirement inherently worsens reasoning.

Declared corrective control
Retain the full public task and final output specification for external evaluation. Route only the explicit final JSON-envelope and field-enumeration prefix out of the internal objective; retain the exact scientific guidance about inlet/STP vs pressure-corrected flow, volume/time closure, stated assumptions, and BLOCK/SCREEN/EXECUTE meanings. Keep native intermediate prompts/schema, production source, cases, inventory, candidate budget, decoding and seeds unchanged. Regenerate all three FlowPilot cases once; reuse all three original one-shot raw outputs, packets and grades. Preserve primary outcomes as the original diagnostic condition. No parser repair or pump-material patch is part of this control. Frozen planning, source hashes and the exact objective diff are created before generation by run_followup.py --phase init.

The control does not promise an advantage for FlowPilot. Persistent independent engineering, chemistry or delivery problems remain in the output and scoring. The comparison is diagnostic and has one generation per case, not independent prospective validation or evidence that token count alone controls performance.
