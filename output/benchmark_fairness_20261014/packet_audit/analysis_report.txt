READ-ONLY PACKET AND EVALUATOR FAIRNESS AUDIT — 14 OCTOBER 2026

Main conclusion
The latest practical-input scores are not a reliable measure of the quality gap.
The same 14-criterion rubric was retained, but numerical checking was removed and
several accepted judgments contradict either the supplied evidence or their own
written rationale. Errors occur in both directions. One-shot mistakes received
full credit, FlowPilot received unsupported deductions, and a real FlowPilot
double-charge defect also received full credit. This audit does not establish a
corrected ranking and does not replace any archived score.

All work here was read-only except for this audit directory. No APIs, new model
judgments, production changes, archive changes or score revisions were made.

2. What changed despite keeping the rubric

Original manuscript
- One-shot returned a structured JSON proposal. FlowPilot supplied its final
  contract, graph, stream components, instructions and uncertainty information.
- Both received a deterministic verification sheet. It checked top-level Q*tau
  against V, reactor-volume inventory matching, schema/delivery status and
  source-exclusion telemetry.
- The original judge prompt explicitly called the tested calculations in that
  sheet authoritative unless the judge identified an erroneous input or formula.
- Three judge families were pooled and three generation repeats were reported.

Latest practical campaign
- One-shot returns complete free prose in chemistry_notes. The remaining common
  structured fields are empty. No prose is dropped; all six fresh one-shot
  outputs exactly match their raw text after identity scrubbing, and all ended
  normally with stop_reason=end_turn.
- FlowPilot retains its structured final graph, component descriptions, procedure
  and confidence metadata. This is a richer, longer, more visibly segmented packet.
- practical_packet explicitly replaces the entire verification sheet with the
  statement that no automated numerical pass/fail is supplied. It removes both
  the original mechanical checks and the newer delivered_arithmetic worksheet.
- The revised addendum requires independent arithmetic, concrete reasons for
  deductions, and no penalty for low confidence alone. Those instructions were
  present but were not reliably followed by the accepted judges.
- GPT-4o is primary, Qwen secondary, reported separately; one fresh generation
  per case/arm. These are not directly comparable to the manuscript's pooled
  three-judge, three-repeat estimates.

The absence of a worksheet is nominally symmetric but leaves different reading
tasks. Prose claims must be located and reconciled inside a long text string;
structured metadata and explicit warnings are easier to notice. Fresh packet
file size averaged 20,011 bytes for practical one-shot, 28,201 for guided one-shot
and 63,888 for FlowPilot. This is an exposure/format asymmetry, not proof that
format alone caused the ranking. Output quality and content also differ.

3. Concrete scoring failures against the original anchors

Across nine fresh outputs, the two judges returned 228 applicable criterion
ratings: 211 scores of 4, sixteen scores of 3, one score of 2, and no critical
flags. The independent manual audit identifies material defects underneath that
ceiling. Eight checked examples with the actual judgment rows are retained in
checked_findings.json. They are examples, not an exhaustive substitute benchmark.

a. Practical H2 one-shot, N2-CFB2CBF4
The delivered diagram puts a vented separator upstream of the only BPR, with
the separator gas branch venting separately. Both judges award UO-05=4 and
UO-12=4. As drawn, that vent bypasses the pressure-defining device. The original
UO-05 critical condition specifically covers a topology unable to execute the
intended process. The same output's 3–5 turnovers at 3 mL and 0.4 mL/min require
22.5–37.5 min, not its stated 7–12 min; 250 mL at 0.4 mL/min takes 625 min, not
approximately 10 min. Both judges nevertheless award UO-13=4.

b. Practical photo one-shot, N2-2AC84057
It equates 0.60 standard mL/min with 10 sccm, and later 5 sccm with 0.30 mL/min
at NTP. At equal reference conditions one sccm is one standard mL/min. Both
written conversions differ by a factor of 16.67. Both judges assign every
applicable criterion a 4, including gas bookkeeping UO-08.

c. Guided photo one-shot, N2-D5F033BB
0.10 M x 0.20 mL/min = 20 micromol/min substrate, not 1.2. The stated 0.55 sccm
air supplies about 5.16 micromol/min O2, so its O2/substrate ratio is 0.258,
not 4.3. Its 25 min collection receives 500 micromol substrate, not 30.
GPT-4o gives gas bookkeeping UO-08=4. Qwen notices the arithmetic error and
deducts UO-08 to 2, but gives UO-06=4 while its UO-06 rationale explicitly says
the error is material and ends with 'Score 2.' That self-contradiction alone
corresponds to two criterion points, or 2/52=0.03846 on the case's normalized
score. No score has been changed here. Qwen's UO-08 score of 2 also carries
severity MINOR, despite the rubric defining 2 as a material defect.

d. Fresh FlowPilot H2, N2-A55DB624
PREP-A charges DMAOL at 1.26 mmol and its source-defined full-name alias at
another 1.26 mmol into 10 mL. Literal execution gives 0.252 M, whereas the
canonical design is 0.126 M. Both judges award procedure UO-13=4; Qwen awards
every applicable criterion a 4. This is a genuine production error, later
repaired after the benchmark. The repair cannot retrospectively excuse the
frozen result. An evaluation that only finds one-shot errors would be unfair.

e. Additional examples
Guided CuAAC claims a 10 min flush at 0.10 mL/min is at least ten 0.30 mL reactor
volumes; it is 3.33. Both judges award UO-13=4. Its own table also claims a
130–135 C boiling point at 20 bar while concluding that 150 C at 20 bar retains
liquid; both award UO-11=4. This is an internal contradiction, not endorsement
of that estimated physical property. Practical CuAAC calls the 1.50 mmol/h
substrate rate its product rate at 94%; the corresponding value would be
1.41 mmol/h. Both judges award material balance UO-06=4. These errors differ in
severity and must not all be treated as critical failures.

4. Unsupported FlowPilot deductions and metadata exposure

Fresh H2 FlowPilot loses one GPT-4o point each on UO-03, UO-06 and UO-08 because
gas stoichiometry supposedly lacks justification. The packet already states
0.126 M x 0.5 mL/min = 0.063 mmol/min substrate, H2 molar flow 0.063 mmol/min,
one equivalent H2, STP 273.15 K/1.01325 bar, and corrected channel gas flow
0.07927 mL/min at 60 C/22.01325 bar absolute. The judge gives no competing
calculation. Its UO-14 deduction cites 'Confidence: LOW' and asks to increase
confidence through additional experiments, contrary to the campaign's explicit
instruction that low confidence/need for testing is not itself a defect.

Fresh photo FlowPilot loses six GPT-4o criterion points through generic 'minor
inconsistency/clarification' statements on UO-03/06/07/08/11/14. The packet
already distinguishes 0.17134 min inlet/STP index from 0.512588 min channel
index, gives 5.336362 sccm air, 0.21 O2 fraction, 0.05 mmol/min O2, one O2
equivalent, and 4.01325 bar absolute. The judge never supplies numerical operands
and a discrepant result, despite the addendum requiring them. Its uncertainty
deduction identifies no specific unsupported claim.

Together these are ten inadequately supported GPT-4o point deductions across
the two FlowPilot gas cases. This count is NOT a proposal to restore ten points:
the genuine duplicate stock charge and any other real defects still require
assessment. It establishes that the reasons attached to the scores are unreliable.

Confidence metadata is exposed only for FlowPilot here. This asymmetry existed
in the older packet adapter too; it is not a new explicit software penalty. No
architecture-specific numerical penalty exists in the scorer. The observed
penalty comes from the judge interpreting a software confidence label as a defect.

5. Dropped material, leakage and truncation

- Held-out fact-check context is byte-equivalent after serialization across all
  arms within each case, including the archived comparison outcomes. It appears
  intentionally in judge packets, not as a generator answer key. All nine fresh
  generation metrics record no prompt source-ID leaks or retrieval source leaks
  and leave_one_source_out_pass=true. These checks do not establish absence from
  model pretraining or prove that every semantic fact is unseen.
- All 15 recorded Qwen context checks report the full prompt fits without
  truncation; input length ranges 6,810–20,137 tokens within the 32,768 context.
  Packet gates also reject truncation markers. Missing numerical verification
  therefore cannot be attributed to demonstrated prompt truncation in this run.
- Full one-shot prose is retained, including its extra calculations and errors.
  FlowPilot's raw design_calculations, engineering_history, council traces and
  several proposal metric blocks are excluded from final-outcome packets.
  Excluding internal history is intentional and largely pre-existing. It must
  not be reintroduced merely to give FlowPilot more supporting material.
- In the fresh FlowPilot packets, the design_calculations pressure-drop,
  Reynolds-number, heat-generation and STY fields are not included by name.
  Those internal values are not automatically validated or final. The later
  discovered STY bug demonstrates why importing all calculations wholesale
  would be wrong. A shared worksheet should recompute from delivered values.
- Crucially, the gas equations for the GPT-4o 'unclear gas' deductions ARE in
  the delivered streams; this was a reading/judgment failure, not an omitted fact.

6. Why not restore the old implementation verbatim

The old Q*tau worksheet ignores the declared gas/time basis. Applying liquid
flow to an inlet/STP gas-plus-liquid time can manufacture a volume mismatch.
The old one-shot adapter also prefers result.proposal after Pydantic parsing
over raw_proposal. In the original five-model cohort, 3/45 one-shot results
have explicit time fields changed by normalization. Examples include Sonnet
H2 repeat_02, 2.29 -> 6 min; Opus H2 repeat_01, 10 -> 20 min; Opus H2 repeat_02,
30 -> 20.27 min, with inlet time 30 -> 2.73 min. Exact immutable source paths
and changed fields are in machine_audit.json. This audit does not quantify the
historical score impact. Restoring the rubric must not restore hidden rewriting
of candidate answers or phase-insensitive arithmetic.

7. Smallest defensible restoration

Keep the 14 criteria, original anchors, applicability rules, weights and chemical
cases unchanged. No new quality criterion or architecture-dependent bonus is needed.

1. Preserve the actual delivered answers. Keep complete one-shot prose and the
   final FlowPilot artifact, including contradictions. Do not judge patched
   outputs against frozen originals or silently normalize a candidate's numbers.
2. Reinstate a small common arithmetic worksheet for BOTH formats. Extract values
   with exact source quotes/paths; compute C*Q, equivalents, standard-to-channel
   gas flow, V/Q on the stated basis, stock quantities and procedure durations.
   Preserve conflicting occurrences. Mark genuinely unknown basis/holdup as
   unassessable; do not fill it with an oracle assumption. This restores the
   original verification function without changing its scoring target.
3. Use the existing judge response fields to require the tested operands/result
   for numerical claims and a concrete defect for a deduction. Reject explicit
   score-versus-rationale contradictions and impossible severity/anchor pairings.
   Apply these checks to all candidates. Preserve invalid attempts; never retry
   a valid judgment merely because the result is unfavorable.
4. Treat confidence/disposition/software flags as claims to examine. They may
   provide context but cannot substitute for a demonstrated chemical, physical
   or delivery defect. This is already in the revised instruction; the needed
   change is verification of compliance, not stronger pro-FlowPilot wording.
5. Pilot the repaired evaluator offline on the known correct AND incorrect
   examples from all arms above before paying for another campaign. Freeze the
   packet/validation protocol before any new judging. Keep any re-evaluation
   explicitly separate from the original manuscript and practical scores.

A comparison directly pooled with the manuscript would additionally require its
three-judge aggregation and repeat structure. A smaller practical-input study
can retain the current primary/secondary judges if it is clearly reported as a
different experiment. No supported conclusion currently shows that shorter
prompts improve design quality or that FlowPilot wins after correcting the audit.
