Production-repair follow-up with frozen one-shot comparators

This is a development evaluation of fixes made after inspecting prior outcomes.
It cannot establish independent validation or historical reproducibility.

Freeze after repairs and offline checks are complete, before any new inference.
Run one FlowPilot generation per case using the same practical public input,
inventory, seed, Claude Sonnet 4.6 generator, candidate/revision budgets and
objective routing as the corrected schema-routing follow-up. Retain all three
attempts, including failures. Do not regenerate a case for its score.

Copy the existing three one-shot outputs, packets and all nine accepted grades
byte-for-byte from claude_fair_bridge_20261014. Never generate, revise or judge
these one-shot outputs again for this follow-up. Both arms keep the same public
facts and inventory. The held-out oracle remains available only in judge context.

Use the unchanged fair_practical_packet adapter, original fourteen criteria,
historical prompts and validators, equally weighted GPT-5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.6
and local Qwen3.6-27B judges. Judge each new FlowPilot output once per judge,
allowing at most three attempts only if the previous response is invalid or
failed. Never retry a valid grade. Do not truncate packets to fit Qwen.

The primary comparison requires all eighteen valid judgments (nine reused,
nine new). Missing grades remain missing, and no primary FlowPilot mean is
reported when its panel is incomplete. Critical flags are reported separately.
No N/A serialization recovery or imputation is applied in this campaign.

The secondary comparison is declared before generation and always uses exactly
GPT-5.4 and Claude, the complete pair used in the preceding sensitivity report.
It requires all twelve paired judgments across the three cases. It is reported
as secondary whether its result is favorable, tied or unfavorable. Qwen is not
included or excluded in response to its ratings.

Paid generation and judging use a separate campaign ledger and a $5 conservative
reservation cap, with the prior campaign's rate schedule and provider routing.
Local GPU cost is unestimated. Transport failures without returned usage can
have unknown provider charges. Do not add further calls to obtain an advantage.

Live source hashes, copies of full source, helper hashes, public inputs, rubric,
judge definitions, frozen source exclusions, baseline grades, telemetry, packets,
raw responses, all validation attempts and accepted results are retained.
Interpret either improvement or decline under this fixed design. Any further
development requires a separately identified campaign, not an overwritten run.
