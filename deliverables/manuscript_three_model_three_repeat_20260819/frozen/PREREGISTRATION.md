# Frozen Campaign

This campaign uses three cases selected before generation from the accepted five-case pilot ranking. It runs Qwen3.6-27B, GPT-5.4, and Claude Sonnet 4.6 as both one-shot and FlowPilot generators, with three fresh calls per cell. Qwen, OpenAI, and Claude independently judge every blinded outcome using the unchanged 14-criterion NewGen 2.0 rubric. Exactly 54 outcomes and 162 judgments are planned.
